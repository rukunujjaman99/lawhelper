Know Your Legal Rights: Maintenance after separation

A wife and children can claim maintenance even after separation. Under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961, the claim is made before the Family Court. Keep proof of marriage, birth certificates and records of income. Ask your lawyer about an interim order if you need support while the case is pending.

Cheque dishonour: what to do in the first 30 days

Under section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, send a legal notice within the time allowed after the bank returns the cheque. Missing a deadline can end your case before it starts. Keep the cheque, the bank memo and the notice receipt together.

Buying land in Dhaka: five documents to check

Check the title deed chain, the mutation record (namjari), the latest khatian, rent receipts for land development tax, and a search at the sub-registry office. Never pay the full price before a lawyer reviews the chain of title.

Your rights when an employer ends your job

The Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 sets notice and benefit rules for termination. Ask for the termination letter in writing and keep your appointment letter and salary records. A labour court complaint has time limits, so speak to a lawyer early.

Online harassment: how to report and preserve evidence

Take dated screenshots that show the profile URL, do not delete messages, and report through the Cyber Security Act complaint process. A lawyer can help you file the complaint at the right tribunal.
